# Smart Electrics logo brief

## Required identity

- symbol plus title-case wordmark `Smart Electrics`;
- no tagline in the primary lockup;
- dark-first copper and warm-amber palette with light and monochrome variants;
- exact architectural geometry with softened corners;
- the idea of a measured plan carrying one controlled electrical impulse;
- legible icon-only forms for favicon and compact navigation.

The mark must avoid literal houses, plugs, bulbs, lightning bolts, generic
shield badges, and decorative “luxury gold” monograms. It should connect rough
electrical infrastructure with later smart-home orchestration without implying
an unsupported vendor or technology.

## Selection process

Three independent throwaway concepts are compared. The Sol orchestrator selects
and refines one production direction based on distinctiveness, small-size
legibility, brand fit, adaptability, and basic web similarity screening. This
screen is not trademark clearance; professional legal review remains necessary
before registration or high-cost rollout.
